# Close Reading

The page is one recorded answer read against its source.

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--ground` | `#0A0A0A` | Ground |
| `--ink` | `#F5F5F2` | Text |
| `--secondary` | `#A3A3A3` | Secondary, about 7.8:1 on the ground |
| `--line` | `#2A2A2A` | Hairlines |
| `--paper` | `#F5F5F2` | Source plane |
| `--paper-ink` | `#111111` | Text on the source plane |
| `--fail` | `#FF4D4D` | Fail, beside the word Fail |

## Type

- Geist 200 for the opening sentence. Geist 300 for section headings. Geist 400 for body.
- Geist Mono 400 for the name and file names, at 16px.
- Instrument Serif italic, once, on the word passage.

Body is 17px. Claims, scores, labels and links are at least 16px. Controls are at least 44px.

## Motion

Threads draw once over 700ms. A verdict settles over 200ms. Reduced motion shows numbered ties and no animation. The stage script pauses when the tab is hidden and does not loop.
